record of the wanderer

An old shell, still walking.

I build software the way old kingdoms were built — one careful chamber at a time, load-bearing and meant to last. Seven years in, I still find the tunnels worth exploring: backend systems, small interactive tools, and the occasional strange side project that has no business existing.

I care about interfaces that don't waste the person on the other end of the screen, code that a stranger could read in the dark, and shipping things that hold their shape under pressure. Currently based between contract work and personal explorations, always looking for the next chamber to map.
